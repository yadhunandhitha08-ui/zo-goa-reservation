    Amenities

    Everything here, and what it costs

    Green is already inside your ₹40,120. Amber is charged only if you want it.

    1 – 3 of 13

    • Breakfast on the terrace

      Poi still warm from the bakery, eggs cooked while you wait and fruit cut that morning. Served 7:30 to 10:30 on the terrace, or brought up to the room.

    • Both Mopa airport runs

      A car each way, booked against your flight number. The driver watches the landing time and waits inside with your name, however late it slips.

    • Infinity pool, steam and gym

      Open from six in the morning until nine at night, towels laid out at the door. The pool looks over the palms to the sea, and is rarely busy before nine.

    • The spa

      Four treatment rooms above the garden. Oil massage from ₹2,100, an hour for two at ₹4,200. The steam room stays inside your rate.

    • A table on the sand at sunset

      A table carried down to the beach and set for the hour you choose. No charge to reserve it. Ask the desk a day ahead, as there are only six.

    • The Care Desk

      One number for a table, a taxi, a doctor or a late arrival. Someone picks up at three in the morning, and a doctor attends in your room.

    • Step-free access

      A lift to every floor and no step into Room 214 or its shower. Grab bars and a fold-down shower seat are fitted at no charge, if you ask before you fly.

    • Travelling with children

      A cot and a high chair at no charge, and the kitchen will cook plain food off the menu. An extra bed for an older child is ₹1,800 a night.

    • Dogs

      Up to 15 kg, in Garden rooms only, at ₹600 a night. A bed and bowls are in the room before you arrive. Not in Ocean-View rooms.

    • The kitchen

      Jain, vegan, gluten-free and low-spice are cooked to order rather than adapted. Tell the kitchen a day ahead and the chef confirms it with you.

    • Parking and valet

      Free on the property, with valet at the door. Covered, and the gate is manned through the night.

    • Laundry and room service

      Laundry comes back the same day if it is in by nine, priced per piece. Room service runs through the night at menu price.

    • Shuttle to Candolim beach

      Every thirty minutes from seven in the morning until eleven at night, to the beach road and back. The walk is six minutes if you would rather.
